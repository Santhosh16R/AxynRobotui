# Axyn Robot Concierge - Facility Knowledge Base

## Building Overview & Hours
- **Facility Name**: Axyn Technologies HQ
- **Operating Hours**: Monday - Friday: 8:00 AM - 7:00 PM. Weekends: Access card only.
- **Main Entrance**: Ground Floor North Entrance.
- **Reception Desk**: Located immediately to your left when entering through the main revolving doors.

## Visitor Services & Amenities
- **Guest Wi-Fi**: Network Name: `Axyn-Guest`, Password: `Welcome2026!`.
- **Restrooms**:
  - Main Restrooms: North Corridor, next to the Elevator Lobby.
  - Accessible Restrooms: East Wing, adjacent to Skyview Cafeteria.
- **Skyview Cafeteria & Lounge**:
  - Breakfast: 8:30 AM – 10:30 AM
  - Lunch: 11:30 AM – 2:30 PM
  - All-day specialty barista coffee, snacks, cold brew, and grab-and-go artisan sandwiches.
- **Water Dispensers & Coffee Stations**: Located in every department wing next to the copy rooms.

## Meeting Rooms & Presentation Spaces
- **Executive Boardroom (Room 101)**: Seats 24 people, dual 85" 4K displays, Polycom video conference suite, high-speed HDMI/AirPlay casting.
- **Auditorium & Demo Theater (Room 105)**: Seats 120 people, full stage audio/lighting, wireless microphones, used for all-hands and product launches.
- **Innovation & Robotics Lab (Room 103)**: Hardware prototyping, sensor calibration, and robot testing grounds. Lab Director: Dr. Sarah Connor.

## Parking & Transit
- **Visitor Parking**: Spaces marked 'V-1' through 'V-20' in the West Underground Garage.
- **Parking Validation**: Available at the Main Reception check-in kiosk.
- **EV Charging Stations**: 8 Level-2 EV charging bays in Lot B.

## Emergency & Safety Guidelines
- **First Aid**: First aid kit and automated external defibrillator (AED) located in the Reception corridor.
- **Emergency Exits**: Clearly marked East and West stairwells leading directly to Assembly Area 1 (Front Lawn).
- **Security Office**: Dial extension 4444 or ask the Axyn Robot Concierge to notify reception.
